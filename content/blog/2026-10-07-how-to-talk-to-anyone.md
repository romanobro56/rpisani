## How to Talk to Anyone
### (and why it's an important skill)

Have you ever wanted to say something to a stranger and chickened out? Maybe you want to talk to more people in general but you don't know how. In any case, fear oftentimes gets in the way of our goals to connect with more people and be free socially. Let me show you exactly how you can delete your mental barrier blocking you from connecting with strangers, and activate the super power of talking to **anybody**. 

First, I want you to understand why being able to talk to anybody is such an important skill. 

1. Connection and Loneliness - In 2021, about half of Americans reported having 3 or fewer close friends, down from 1 in 4 in 1990<sup class="note-ref cite"><a href="#note-1" id="ref-1" aria-label="Note 1">[1]</a></sup>

2. The "first word" principle: think - every person you know today - all of your friends, your favorite person, was once a stranger. Some people you know may have been familial connections, friend of a friend - sure. Many also weren't. And if nobody ever made a cold intro, friendships would cease to exist altogether.

3. Inquiring has essentially **zero** downside. If you approach a total stranger with good intentions, jumble all your words and make a fool of yourself, nothing bad will happen to you. You walk away with nothing, which is the same as if you never said a word. This is to say, most, if not all of the percieved danger of talking to someone new is made up inside your head.
